About Me

Hi! My name is Kyann.

Besides school and my friends, Euro Truck Simulator is a big part of my life. I first discovered it in 2016, but I only really got back into it in late 2024, after building my own PC. When I joined TruckersMP, I was amazed by the sheer size of its community. Within a few days, I joined a VTC and made lots of friends. Since then, TruckersMP has become much more than just a game for me: it’s a true passion and a community I’m proud to be part of!

My VTC

Jastuk

Virtual Trucking Company, since 2026

About Jastuk

Jastuk was founded on April 23, 2026 by JustNico and Luca. Their goal is to build an inclusive, international community of drivers who share a passion for the roads of Europe and America.

It’s more than a VTC. It’s a family, with organized convoys, events and a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

Our mission: to make every driver feel at home, in a warm place where everyone belongs.

Key dates

  1. April 23, 2026Founded
  2. April 29, 2026Validated
  3. August 21, 2026Verified

To join

  • Be at least 16 years old
  • Have a TruckersMP account at least 3 months old
  • Have fewer than 4 active bans
  • Join the Discord server

What Jastuk offers

  • Drivers HubEvents, distance driven, jobs and leaderboards in one place.
  • Job TrackerTracks your jobs, distance and earnings.
  • DiscordPlenty of chat channels, driver-only channels and custom roles.
  • ManagementA team that keeps the VTC running and supports drivers.
  • ConvoysWeekly convoys for drivers and monthly public convoys.
  • CommunityFriendly drivers who help the community grow.

My role
